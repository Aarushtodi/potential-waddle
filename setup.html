Tally setup
Add the Tally bookmark

Your grades stay on your phone. Tally never asks for your password.

Then follow the steps for your phone.

Once: put Tally on your Home Screen
  1. Open Tally in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. iPhone: tap •••, then Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Android: tap the three dots menu, then Add to Home screen, then Install.
Once: add the bookmark on iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap Copy the bookmark code at the top of this page.
  2. Tap ••• at the bottom of Safari, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add Bookmark. Name it Tally grades, pick Favorites, tap Save.
  4. Tap ••• again, then Bookmarks.
  5. Tap Edit, then tap Tally grades.
  6. Tap the web address under the name, clear it, long press, tap Paste.
  7. Tap Done, then Done.
Once: add the bookmark on Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap Copy the bookmark code at the top of this page.
  2. Tap the three dots menu at the top right, then the star.
  3. Tap Edit on the note at the bottom. If it is gone: three dots menu, Bookmarks, the three dots next to the bookmark, Edit.
  4. Change the name to Tally grades.
  5. Clear the web address, long press it, tap Paste.
  6. Tap the back arrow to save.
Each time: new grades on iPhone
  1. Open HAC in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap •••, then Bookmarks, then Favorites, then Tally grades.
  3. Tap Copy grades.
  4. Open Tally from your Home Screen, long press the box, tap Paste.
Each time: new grades on Android
  1. Open HAC in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap the address bar, type Tally grades, tap the starred Tally grades.
  3. Tap Copy grades.
  4. Open Tally from your Home Screen, long press the box, tap Paste.
Copy did not work?

Long press the code, tap Select All, then Copy.
